Peasant Funnel Muster

Random level-0 commoners for a 5e funnel. 3d6 in order, no rerolls, no mercy.

House rules baked in: HP is 1d4 + Con (minimum 1). AC is 10 + Dex. Each peasant is proficient (+2) with their trade weapon only. Omens work once per life. Click Dead to strike a name from the roll; survivors after the funnel pick a class at 1st level.
